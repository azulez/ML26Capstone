Transcript Format Examples

The model splits transcript text into speaker turns using a simple heuristic parser (src/modeling/turns.py). It expects Motley-Fool-style formatting, not arbitrary dialogue notation -- the examples below are all known to parse successfully. Click "Copy" and paste one into the main page to try it.

Header line rules (a line naming who's speaking, followed by their turn's text on the next line(s)):
  • Must contain a literal " -- " (space, two hyphens, space) separating the speaker from their title, e.g. Jane Doe -- Chief Executive Officer
  • Must start with an uppercase letter
  • Must not end in sentence punctuation (. ? ! , : ;) -- this is the most common mistake: "CEO: some text" is not recognized as a header
  • Operator and Interviewer are also recognized as bare, title-less headers on their own line
Anything that doesn't match a header is treated as body text for whichever speaker's turn is currently open -- a transcript with zero valid headers parses to zero turns and the API returns "No speaker turns found".

1. Minimal

The smallest input that parses: one header, one line of text.

Jane Doe -- Chief Executive Officer
This is a smoke test.

2. Multiple speakers

Each new header starts a new turn; role is inferred from the title (classify_role in src/modeling/turns.py) -- here, CEO and CFO.

Jane Doe -- Chief Executive Officer
Thank you for joining us today. Revenue grew 12% year over year.

John Smith -- Chief Financial Officer
Our margins expanded due to cost discipline this quarter.

3. Full shape: sections, Operator, and an analyst

Section markers (a short line ending in :, e.g. Prepared Remarks:) don't become turns themselves -- they just tag the turns that follow. Operator is a bare header with no title. Analysts often get a three-part header (Name -- Firm -- Analyst); everything after the first " -- " becomes the title, so role classification still finds "Analyst" in it.

Prepared Remarks:
Jane Doe -- Chief Executive Officer
Thank you for joining us today. Revenue grew 12% year over year.

John Smith -- Chief Financial Officer
Our margins expanded due to cost discipline this quarter.

Questions and Answers:
Operator
Our first question comes from the line of Alex Analyst.

Alex Analyst -- Big Bank -- Analyst
Can you comment on guidance for next quarter?

Jane Doe -- Chief Executive Officer
We remain optimistic about the back half of the year.
